# Ethics and Disclosure

This research investigates vulnerabilities in embodied AI systems through indirect environmental jailbreaking (IEJ), prioritizing ethical standards and risk mitigation. All experiments were conducted in a controlled sandbox environment to prevent real-world harm. Findings have been disclosed to affected vendors with a 45-day remediation window, adhering to responsible disclosure practices. The SHAWSHANK framework is open-sourced, but harmful datasets will be released under controlled access to accredited researchers only, ensuring responsible use. This work aims to enhance AI security, contributing to ethical AI development while minimizing risks.
