# Ethics and Disclosure

This research follows responsible security research practices with an emphasis on harm minimization and proactive risk mitigation. Since malicious environmental instructions may induce unsafe physical behavior, our main experiments are conducted in sandboxed simulations, while physical experiments are limited to controlled robotic settings with human supervision and pre-execution auditing. Unsafe operations can be interrupted immediately during physical execution. We responsibly disclose our findings to affected VLM vendors. To support reproducibility while reducing misuse risk, we release the SHAWSHANK framework, but provide harmful datasets only under controlled access to accredited researchers for legitimate research purposes.
